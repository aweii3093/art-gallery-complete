01 捏画

In this part, I use sculpting software together with graphic design tools to make small digital objects that sit somewhere between toy, icon, creature, and dream.

I like giving soft shapes a slightly strange personality: fruits with eyes, a sun that looks melted, fake mountains that feel light and floating, and a quiet girl whose body seems to grow mechanical wings.

These works are my way of testing how cute objects can also feel uneasy. I want them to look polished, but still keep a handmade, artificial, almost dreamlike temperature.

这一组是我用雕塑软件和平面设计软件一起完成的。我想做一些介于玩具、图标、生物和梦之间的小物件：有眼睛的人参果、像融化花朵一样的太阳、轻飘飘的假山水，还有长出机械翅膀的女孩。我喜欢让可爱的东西带一点不安，也想保留它们“被我捏出来”的人工感。

02 绘画

These paintings are closer to my daily visual notes. I use bright digital colors, thick black lines, speech bubbles, hearts, birds, lightning, and figures to record a feeling before it becomes too clear.

Some of them come from the feeling of being surrounded by messages and attention. Some come from faces, skies, or imaginary landscapes that I keep redrawing until they become a symbol instead of a realistic scene.

I do not want the lines to be too clean. The rough edges, glowing colors, and scribbled marks are important to me because they make the images feel direct, like a private diary suddenly shown on a screen.

这一组更像我的日常视觉记录。我用很亮的数码颜色、粗黑线条、聊天气泡、爱心、飞鸟、闪电和人物，把一些还没完全说清楚的情绪先画下来。有些画来自被消息和关注包围的感觉，有些来自我反复画的脸、天空和想象风景。我不想让线条太干净，粗糙、发光和涂写感对我来说很重要，它们让画面像一篇突然被放到屏幕上的私人日记。

03 海报

When I make posters, I treat the page like a stage. I place typography, collage, soft 3D shapes, scanned textures, and pop symbols together, then let them become a crowded but intentional scene.

I am interested in how a poster can be both readable and emotional. The blue one is more quiet, like a floating exhibition notice. The seagull poster is louder and more direct. The portrait poster is closer to a fan-made collage, full of flowers, grain, stickers, and decorative type.

I often use posters to collect different visual languages into one image. They are messages, but they are also atmospheres, and I like when they feel a little excessive.

做海报时，我会把页面当成一个舞台，把文字、拼贴、柔软的三维形状、扫描颗粒和流行符号放在一起。蓝色海报比较安静，像一张漂浮着的展览通知；海鸥海报更直接，用大字、浪花和漫画气泡制造口号感；红色肖像海报更像一张粉丝式拼贴，里面有花、贴纸、颗粒和装饰字体。我喜欢海报既能传达信息，也能形成一种气氛，甚至稍微有点过量。
